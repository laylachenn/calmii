# calmii

A small, calming hub for anxious moments: a breathing aura, a toolkit, a drift game, and quick YouTube/Twitch links.

- `web/index.html`: the whole app (HTML/CSS/JS, works offline). You can also open it straight in a browser.
- `mac/main.swift`: the native Mac wrapper: app window, menu bar icon, and desktop widget.
- `./build.sh` builds `build/Calmii.app`. `./build.sh install` also copies it to `~/Applications`.

Desktop widget: drag to move, click to open breathing, right-click for options. You can also turn it on or off from the menu bar icon or with ⌘D.
